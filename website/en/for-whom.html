For whom?

Is CederStem right for your family?

CederStem is for children aged 0 to 12 and those around them. Do you recognise your child or student in one of the situations below? Then you're welcome for a no-obligation conversation.

Learning difficulties

Such as dyslexia, dyscalculia or other specific learning disorders — support that fits how your child learns.

Developmental difficulties

Delayed or atypical development in language, motor skills, social-emotional functioning or behaviour.

Multilingual newcomers

Children who have recently arrived in Belgium and are growing up between multiple languages and cultures.

Children with an intellectual disability

Tailored support, with attention to independence, wellbeing and growth opportunities.

Parents

You have questions or concerns about your child's development and are looking for a clear, warm point of contact.

Teachers & care professionals

You support a child with specific needs and would like advice or collaboration on the approach in class.

Good to know

Working with the wider network

With your consent, CederStem is happy to collaborate with school, the CLB (student guidance centre), Kind en Gezin, and other care providers around your child. That way, support doesn't stand apart from the rest of your child's life, but connects with it.

Reimbursement options through your health insurance fund (mutualiteit) are assessed case by case, depending on the fund and type of programme. Feel free to ask during the introductory conversation.

Not sure if CederStem fits your situation?

A short conversation often provides clarity. Feel free to get in touch, with no obligation.
