Approach

A five-step journey.

Orthopedagogical support means working together with the child, the parents and their environment to find what a child needs to grow — at home, at school and in daily life. At CederStem, that happens in five clear steps.

01

Introduction

A no-obligation, low-threshold conversation — by phone or in person — where we get to know each other and discuss your question and your child's situation.

02

Observation & analysis

I map out your child's strengths and needs, if useful in consultation with school, the CLB (Belgian student guidance centre) or other care providers (with your consent).

03

Tailored support plan

Together with you, I set achievable, concrete goals, matched to your child's pace and talents.

04

Individual guidance

Regular, playful, multisensory sessions in which your child grows step by step — with attention to enjoyment and self-confidence.

05

Follow-up & evaluation

We regularly evaluate progress, adjust where needed, and refer to specialised help if that is appropriate.

This five-step plan is a working draft based on your briefing. Let us know if you'd like the journey named or structured differently — we'll refine the text and structure together.

What is orthopedagogical support?

Looking at the whole child

More than the difficulty alone

Orthopedagogical support doesn't just look at what's difficult, but at the child as a whole: their talents, family, school and environment.

Collaboration is central

Parents, school, the CLB and other care providers work together — always with the child at the centre of the approach.
