Privacy policy

Handling your data with care.

This privacy policy is a draft, prepared as a starting point in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Belgian privacy law. Have this reviewed by a lawyer or DPO before the site goes live — especially since health-related data about children is processed.

1. Who is responsible?

CederStem, [name of data controller], [address], [Belgian company number KBO/BCE], is responsible for processing personal data via this website. For privacy questions, contact cederstem@gmail.com.

2. What data do we collect?

  • Contact details you provide via the contact form: name, email address, phone number, your child's age, and the content of your message.
  • As part of a support programme: sensitive data about your child's development, learning and wellbeing (a special category of personal data under GDPR Art. 9). This is only processed with explicit consent and only to the extent necessary for the support.
  • Technical data (e.g. via functional cookies) to make the website work properly.

3. Why do we process this data?

We use your data to answer your question, organise and follow up on a support programme for your child, and to comply with legal obligations. The legal basis is always your consent (Art. 6.1.a and Art. 9.2.a GDPR) or the performance of the support agreement.

4. Photos and imagery

Photos or videos of children are never used (e.g. on the website or social media) without explicit, written consent from the parent(s) or guardian, in line with image rights.

5. How long do we keep data?

Contact form data is kept for as long as needed to answer your question. Records from a support programme are kept in accordance with applicable retention periods for care/support files [period to confirm, e.g. 10 years], and deleted or anonymised afterwards.

6. Who do we share data with?

Only with your explicit consent is data shared with third parties such as school, the CLB, Kind en Gezin, or other care providers, and only to the extent relevant for supporting your child. We never sell data to third parties.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time. You can contact us via cederstem@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (GBA/APD).

8. Cookies

This website only uses functional cookies necessary for the site to work (e.g. remembering your cookie preference). No tracking or advertising cookies are placed without your prior consent.

9. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss or unlawful processing. The contact form does not send your data to any third party: submitting it opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, addressed to cederstem@gmail.com. You send the message yourself, from your own email account.
