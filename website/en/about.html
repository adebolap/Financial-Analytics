About me

A short introduction.

My name is Patience Afram, orthopedagogical support worker and founder of CederStam. I support children aged 0 to 12 with learning or developmental difficulties, and their parents, with as much patience as expertise.

My story

After years of experience in [organisation / school / practice — to complete], I saw how much of a difference it makes when a child receives support that is truly tailored to them: not focused only on the difficulty, but on the whole child — their talents, their pace, their environment.

That conviction is at the heart of CederStam. The name refers to the cedar tree: a tree that roots deep, bends with the wind, and still stands tall. That is exactly how I want to support children — with solid ground beneath their feet, and room to grow at their own pace.
