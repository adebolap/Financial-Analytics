Frequently asked questions

Still have questions?

What happens during the introduction?

A no-obligation conversation (by phone or in person) where we discuss your question and your child's situation. There's no obligation to start afterwards.

Which languages are available?

CederStam is available in Dutch and English.

Where do the sessions take place?

Sessions are possible in person, as home visits, or online, in Antwerp and the surrounding area.

How long does a support programme take?

That varies from child to child. Together we set achievable goals and regularly evaluate whether adjustments are needed.

Does CederStam work with school?

Yes, if desired and always with your consent.

What does a session cost?

Rates are discussed during the introduction, tailored to your programme. Payment can be made by bank transfer or after the session.

Ready to get in touch?

Feel free to reach out for a no-obligation conversation about your child and your situation.
